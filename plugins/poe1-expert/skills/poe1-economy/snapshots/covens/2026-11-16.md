**Trend and main archetypes**
- **Melee strikes and slams lead**, mostly on Slayer: Static Strike and Shockwave together are about one character in eight, though both shares are falling as players move off their starters.
- **Minions are growing**: Necromancer rose from 10% to 12%, and Animate Guardian, Animate Weapon of Ranged Arms and Raise Spectre all sit in the top twelve.
- **Crit is taking over as gear arrives**: 51% of main skills are tagged crit against 31% on day 1; non-crit fell from 33% to 18%.
- **Damage types**: lightning 26%, chaos 22% (up from 15%), fire 19%, physical 14%, cold 13%.
- **Totems** are a steady share (11%, up from 8%), mostly Hierophant; **mines** (3%) and **traps** (2%) are niche.
- **Righteous Fire** (Chieftain and others) and **Poisonous Concoction of Bouncing** (Pathfinder) hold steady as the fire and chaos starters.

This is popularity, not strength: a popular skill is not proof it is the best choice for a given player.
