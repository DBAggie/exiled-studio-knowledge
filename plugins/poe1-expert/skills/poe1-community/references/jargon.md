# PoE1 jargon and abbreviations

Each entry is one line in our own words. Slang drifts between leagues; when a term
names a system, check that the system still exists in 3.30 (see `atlas.md` and
`crafting.md`). Entries marked **PoE2 differs** use a name PoE2 also uses with other
rules. Do not apply the PoE1 meaning there.

## Defence and survival
- **EHP**: effective health pool. How much raw damage you can take before dying, after mitigation. Always ask "against which damage type".
- **ES**: energy shield. A second pool on top of life that recharges after a delay without damage.
- **CI**: Chaos Inoculation. Keystone: life fixed at 1, immune to chaos damage. The build then lives on ES.
- **LL / low life**: running with life reserved down to "low life" on purpose, usually to turn on low-life bonuses. Usually paired with ES.
- **MoM**: Mind over Matter. Keystone that takes part of the damage from mana before life. **PoE2 differs**; do not apply this there.
- **Eldritch Battery (EB)**: keystone that turns ES into extra mana for costs.
- **RT**: Resolute Technique. Keystone: hits never miss, but you can't crit.
- **Max res / overcap**: resist above the cap (75% by default). Overcap only matters when something lowers your resists.
- **Spell supp / supp**: spell suppression. A chance to take less damage from spells.
- **Block / spell block**: chance to block attacks and spells, two separate stats.
- **Phys taken as**: modifiers that move part of incoming physical damage to an elemental type, so resists apply to it.
- **Flasks up / flask uptime**: how much of the time your flasks are active. Often matters more than raw numbers.
- **One-shot**: dying to one hit from full. The test every endgame build must pass against bosses.
- **Degen**: damage over time you take, such as burning ground or a curse.

## Offence
- **DPS**: damage per second. Community numbers quoted from PoB or our engine assume a fixed config; ask what it is.
- **DoT**: damage over time (ignite, poison, bleed, degen spells).
- **Crit / CoC**: critical strike. CoC is Cast on Critical Strike, a trigger support.
- **CwC / CwDT**: Cast while Channelling; Cast when Damage Taken. Trigger supports.
- **Self-cast**: you cast the spell yourself, not through a trigger.
- **RF**: Righteous Fire. A burning aura that damages you and nearby enemies.
- **Pen**: penetration. Reduces the enemy's resist for your hits only.
- **Exposure**: a debuff that lowers one enemy resist.
- **Conversion**: changing one damage type to another before scaling.
- **Clear speed**: how fast a build kills packs while mapping.
- **Bossing / single target**: how well a build kills one tough enemy.

## Gear and links
- **6L / 5L / 4L**: an item with six (five, four) linked sockets. A 6L main skill is the usual endgame target.
- **Corrupted / vaaled**: an item changed by a Vaal Orb; it can no longer be changed by most crafting.
- **Implicit / explicit**: implicit mods sit above the line and come from the base or special sources; explicit mods are the affixes.
- **Prefix / suffix**: the two affix slots. A rare holds up to three of each.
- **T1 mod**: the highest tier of a modifier. Not the same as a T1 map.
- **ilvl**: item level. Decides which mod tiers can roll.
- **Item tier (3.30)**: a new 2 to 5 grade on rare and magic drops from Act 6 on. It raises mod tiers, quality and socket chances. Different from mod tier and map tier.
- **Fractured**: a mod locked in place; crafting can't remove it.
- **Influenced**: an item with Shaper, Elder or Conqueror influence and their special mods.
- **Synth / synthesised**: items with synthesis implicits.
- **Foulborn**: unique variants with a changed mod, introduced in 3.27.
- **Cluster jewel / timeless jewel**: special jewels that add passive nodes or rewrite nodes in a radius.
- **Watcher's Eye, Forbidden pair**: well-known chase jewels. Name examples only; look up values with tools.

## Currency and trade
- **c / chaos**: Chaos Orb. The small trade unit. **PoE2 differs**; do not apply its PoE1 effect there.
- **div / d**: Divine Orb. The main trade unit for valuable items in current PoE1.
- **ex**: Exalted Orb. Once the top currency; now a mid-value orb that adds one mod. Exalts became more common in 3.28. **PoE2 differs**; there the Exalted Orb is the main trade unit.
- **mirror / mirror tier**: Mirror of Kalandra, the rarest orb; "mirror tier" means an item close to perfect.
- **Alch, alt, aug, regal, scour, annul**: the basic crafting orbs by short name.
- **Binding**: Orb of Binding. Can no longer be obtained in 3.30; existing ones still work.
- **Currency Exchange**: the in-game exchange for currency, with a gold fee.
- **Gold**: a second currency, used for fees and Kingsmarch workers.
- **Bulk**: buying or selling many of one item at once, often at a discount.
- **Price fix / price check (PC)**: a misleading listing; asking what something is worth.
- **Flip / flipping**: buying low and selling high as a way to earn.
- **Tujen / Rog / Kirac**: NPC shops and vendors named by their character.

## League and modes
- **League start / LS**: the first days of a new challenge league, from a fresh character.
- **SC / HC**: softcore and hardcore. Death in HC sends the character to the parent softcore league.
- **SSF**: solo self-found. No trading or parties.
- **HCSSF**: hardcore and SSF together.
- **Ruthless**: a harsher ruleset with fewer drops and its own balance. Advice for normal leagues often does not hold there.
- **Standard**: the permanent league. Old items exist there that can't drop now.
- **Leaguestart build / starter**: a build that works with little gear. See `archetypes.md`.
- **Respec**: changing passive points, with Orbs of Regret or the reset quests.
- **Meta**: what most players run this league. Snapshot content; never in this file.

## Mapping and atlas
- **Maps / T1 to T16**: endgame areas in tiers. White 1 to 5, yellow 6 to 10, red 11 to 16. **PoE2 differs** (waystones); do not apply this there.
- **T17 / Nightmare Maps**: the tier above red. Reworked as Nightmare Maps in 3.28.
- **Atlas / atlas tree**: the endgame map grid and its own passive tree. In 3.30 the tree starts in the centre.
- **Voidstones (stones)**: endgame items that raise map tiers on the atlas. "Four stoned" means all four done.
- **Juice / juicing**: stacking map modifiers, rarity, pack size and extra content to get more loot per map.
- **Threads**: the 3.30 map-device consumables that replaced scarabs and add league content.
- **Scarabs**: the pre-3.30 map consumables. Removed in 3.30; existing ones were converted to Threads.
- **Atlas Drops**: the 3.30 replacement for item quantity on map mods. It raises map and Thread drops.
- **Rarity / IIR**: increased item rarity. In 3.30 it also picks which drop pools a monster can roll.
- **Pack size**: more monsters per pack, more loot by count.
- **Sustain**: getting back as many maps as you spend.
- **Alch and go**: run maps with a cheap roll and no extra investment.
- **Rippy mods**: map mods that make a build die, such as reflect or no regen. Reflect became thorns in 3.29.
- **Blasting / blaster**: mapping very fast for clear, not bosses.
- **Pinnacle / uber**: the hardest bosses and their harder versions.
- **Carry / boss carry**: paying another player to kill a boss for you.
- **Delirium, Breach, Legion, Abyss, etc.**: league mechanics, named by the league they came from.

## Common mistakes
- Treating "T1" as one thing. A T1 mod is the best roll; a T1 map is the worst tier; a 3.30 item tier is a drop grade.
- Saying "ex" means the top currency. In PoE1 it is the divine; in PoE2 the exalt.
- Talking about scarabs, sextants or binding orbs as current. Scarabs left in 3.30; Bindings no longer drop.
- Assuming a league mechanic name means it still spawns in maps by itself. In 3.30 primary mechanics need Threads.
- Mixing PoE2 terms (waystones, spirit, the PoE2 exalt) into PoE1 answers.
- Quoting a DPS or EHP number without the config behind it.
