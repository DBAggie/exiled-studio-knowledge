# Atlas progression and farming

Concepts for the PoE1 endgame as of 3.30. 3.30 changed the atlas more than most
patches: scarabs are gone, league content no longer spawns in maps by itself, and the
atlas tree was rebuilt. Advice from 3.29 or older guides is often wrong now. What is
most profitable this league is snapshot content (`poe1-economy`). PoE2's atlas,
waystones and towers differ; do not apply this there.

## Map tiers
- Tiers 1 to 5 are white, 6 to 10 yellow, 11 to 16 red.
- **Tier 17 (Nightmare Maps)**: reworked in 3.28. Each Nightmare Map you complete
  gives one atlas passive point. They drop once Voidstones allow it. 3.30 moved the
  bench meta-craft recipes into them.
- 3.28: a map item no longer belongs to one fixed area.
- 3.30: several maps returned to the atlas and many left.

## Progression in order
1. **Finish the campaign** and reach the atlas.
2. **White and yellow maps**: complete new maps for atlas points and bonus objectives.
   Keep map sustain going.
3. **Red maps and the Conquerors / Sirus**: unlock the higher tiers.
4. **Searing Exarch, Eater of Worlds, Maven, Shaper, Elder, Uber Elder**: atlas points
   and **Voidstones**. 3.30: Maven, the Exarch, the Eater and each of the Maven's six
   special invitations give 2 atlas points (and respec points); Uber Elder gives 4.
5. **Four Voidstones**: since 3.28, each Voidstone sets its quadrant's minimum tier to
   16, makes maps drop one tier higher, and turns tier 15 and lower drops into gold.
   One more Voidstone comes from the Incarnation of Dread.
6. **Nightmare Maps and uber bosses**.

## The atlas passive tree (3.30)
- Reworked: you start in the centre and path outward.
- Total points raised to 150 (from 138).
- **Primary league clusters** (Abyss, Breach, Legion and so on) were reworked.
- **Secondary clusters** (Essence, Harvest, Mercenaries, Ore Deposits, Rogue Exiles,
  Shrines, Strongboxes, Torment and others) were reworked or added.
- **Tertiary clusters** only raise how often a random mechanic appears.
- Delve and Heist clusters were removed.
- Several keystones were removed; Unwavering Vision was reworked; a new keystone,
  Phantom Anomalies, was added. Its exact effect is not described here; look it up.

Plan the tree around one or two farming targets. Splitting points across many
mechanics usually earns less.

## Threads (replacing scarabs, 3.30)
- **Scarabs are removed.** Existing scarabs became matching Threads or Frayed Threads.
- There are ten Thread types, each tied to a primary mechanic: Abyss, Bestiary,
  Betrayal, Blight, Breach, Expedition, Incursion, Legion, Ritual, Ultimatum.
- You spend Threads at the map device. Each Thread adds its mechanic to the map.
- Up to eight can be spent per map. More Threads raise the chance of strong
  "transformative" effects. Unwavering Vision lets you go two over the cap.
- **Frayed Thread** gives a random Thread.
- Threads drop more with Atlas Drops, from Exarch and Eater altar mods, and Shaped
  Regions give free ones.

## League mechanics as farming targets
- 3.30: the ten primary mechanics **no longer spawn randomly** in maps. To farm one,
  you bring its Threads.
- Tertiary mechanics (Beyond, Buried Sentinels, Delirium mirrors, Mirage, Viridian
  Wildwood) can still appear randomly; atlas clusters raise their chance.
- Mirage became a core mechanic in 3.30 (it was the 3.28 league).
- Mercenaries became core in 3.29 and have atlas support.
- Harvest was reworked in 3.30 (Sacred Flora and Fauna).
- Delirium orbs now make the map delirious instead of adding an enchant (3.30).
- Pick a target by what your build kills well: fast clear for Breach and Legion style
  density, a sturdy build for Ultimatum or Ritual waves, bossing for Incursion or
  boss-heavy targets. Whether a target pays this league is snapshot content.

## Shaped Regions and Astrolabes
- An Astrolabe makes a Shaped Region in one quadrant, changing many maps at once.
- 3.28: clearing the region's added content leads to a Memory Vault.
- 3.30: Shaped Regions come in ten types, each tied to a Thread type.

## Anomalies and Valdo
- 3.29: completing Voidstone-affected maps can create Anomalies on the atlas. They
  need no map item and ignore atlas passives and fragments.
- Valdo's Puzzle Box gives foiled Tier 17 maps; 3.30 added more options.

## Juicing
Juicing means stacking ways to get more loot per map. The levers in 3.30:
- **Map mods**: more mods means more pack size, rarity and Atlas Drops, and more risk.
  3.30: one corruption outcome now adds two explicit mods (to a maximum of nine)
  instead of rerolling the map.
- **Atlas Drops** (3.30): replaces item quantity on map mods at the same values. It
  raises map and Thread drops, not all items.
- **Item rarity** (3.30): on monsters and chests it also decides which drop pools can
  roll, so higher rarity gives more valuable currency, not just more of it.
- **Threads**: more league content per map, up to eight.
- **Delirium**: makes the map delirious.
- **Eldritch altars**: choose good altar mods. 3.30 lowered their rarity bonus.
- **Fragments**: 3.30: Shaper, Elder and Conqueror fragments no longer add quantity.
- **Kingsmarch**: workers can run maps and ship goods for currency (core since 3.26).

Juicing only pays when the build can clear the content safely. Dying loses the map.
Start light and add one lever at a time.

## The Covens of Umbra league (3.30)
The challenge league adds covenites, Fates that empower area bosses (Grim Fates on
high-level bosses), and Viscera that strengthen the Wickerman. Its farming value is
snapshot content. Rules may change with 3.30 hotfixes; check the latest notes.

## Common mistakes
- Recommending scarabs or "scarab juicing". Scarabs were removed in 3.30.
- Saying Breach, Legion or Abyss appear in maps by chance. In 3.30 they need Threads.
- Using "item quantity" on maps. It is Atlas Drops in 3.30, with a narrower effect.
- Treating Tier 17 as the pre-3.28 version. They are Nightmare Maps now.
- Quoting the old atlas tree layout or point total (138).
- Expecting fragments to add quantity to a map in 3.30.
- Using PoE2 atlas, waystone or tower rules.
